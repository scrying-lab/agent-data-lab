DATA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Bike Sharing dataset (data/hour.csv), included unmodified
  Fanaee-T, H. (2013). Bike Sharing [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
  https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Original research: Hadi Fanaee-T and Joao Gama, "Event labeling combining
  ensemble detectors and background knowledge".
  The lab computes its own grouped summaries (mean rentals and observed hours)
  from the unmodified file.
